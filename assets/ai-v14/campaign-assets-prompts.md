# v1.4 Bright Campaign Asset Batch

This batch is generated with the built-in image generation tool and kept as
assets only. Existing `assets/stage-intros/*.png` files are not overwritten.

## Shared Style Prompt

Japanese Tactical HD-2D, bright Japanese console-game key art, hand-painted
anime military fantasy, fictional unmanned tanks, no embedded UI text, no logo,
no watermark, no real-world insignia.

## Stage Intro Assets

- `title-bright.png`: heroic morning campaign panorama after rain, wet road, distant fortress, friendly tank convoy.
- `stage-01-desert-bright.png`: warm desert frontline, gold sunrise, red-brown mesas, sandbags and distant outpost.
- `stage-02-rain-bright.png`: cold rain battlefield, blue-silver puddles, bunkers, unmanned armored units.
- `stage-03-forest-bright.png`: deep green forest, sunlight shafts, mossy ruins and tank route.
- `stage-04-industrial-bright.png`: industrial ruins, gray concrete, rust-orange pipes, steam and bright daylight.
- `stage-05-snow-bright.png`: snow battlefield, blue-white reflection, icy road and winter fortifications.
- `stage-06-fortress-bright.png`: luminous night fortress, searchlights, moonlight, blue lightning and wet stone road.
- `stage-07-final-bright.png`: final battlefield at dawn, red firelight, lava cracks and distant final fortress.

## Terrain Assets

- `stage-05-snow-ground.png`: snow, ice patches, compacted tracks, small rocks and frozen cracks.
- `stage-06-fortress-ground.png`: wet fortress slabs, steel grates, cyan light seams and moonlit reflections.
